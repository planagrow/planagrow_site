How it works

Five steps from a photo to a planted plan.

The app follows the same path every gardener does, with the measuring and the plant research done for you.

  1. 1Photo

    Photograph your yard

    Take a picture of the space you plan to plant with your phone. Then use the app to find your hardiness zone from your ZIP code.

  2. 2Layout

    Draw your beds

    The top-down grid shows your garden's layout. Draw an outline of the beds you are working on, to scale, so every plant fits.

  3. 3Plants

    Choose what fits

    Then make your own personal catalog. Answer a few quick questions about light and space, and pick from plants that suit your beds.

  4. 4See it

    See it grown

    See your new bed with the plants you selected, arranged the way you want them. The app gives you hints to make it even better.

  5. 5Optional

    Share, if you like

    Show your design to the gardening community, or keep it private. It is your choice, and you can change your mind at any time.

Plant cards

One card for every plant, and every fact has a source.

  • Facts from universities and public agencies. We use extension services such as NC State's, and the USDA. When they disagree, the card says so.
  • Useful where you live. Add your ZIP code and each card tells you whether the plant suits your hardiness zone, and whether it grows as an annual or a perennial where you are.
  • Sizes you can plan with. How big it gets, and our estimate of its size a few years after planting.
  • Careful about safety. Where a plant can harm people or pets, the card says so and names the source. We never call a plant safe.
  • Free, always. The plant library and every plant card cost nothing to use.

What we stand for

Information you can trust.

Our mission

Helping everyone who loves gardening make a plan that suits where they live: the right plant in the right place, seen fully grown before it goes in the ground. Free, sourced and independent.

PlanaGrow Group is a small, independent company. We paint every plant to scale and check every fact with university and government sources, so you choose a plant for the size it will become, not the size it is in the pot.

A plant that suits its spot needs less replacing and less water, and gives more back to bees, butterflies and birds. Choosing well at the start is the most sustainable thing a gardener can do.
